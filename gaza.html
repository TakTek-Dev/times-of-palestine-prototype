Health11 Nov

Red Crescent: infections rising after Gaza floods

The Red Crescent's Bashar Murad says about 70,000 tents were flooded and respiratory infections and meningitis cases are increasing.

Displacement8 Nov

Sand cliff collapse kills five in Deir al-Balah camp

A sand cliff loosened by rain fell on tents at a Deir al-Balah displacement site on 8 November, killing five people including three children.

Winter storm8 Nov

First winter storm swamps Gaza's displacement sites

OCHA says rain from 6 November destroyed over 6,000 family shelters, damaged 17,400 more and left 3,900 households with none.

Courts5 Nov

Courts refuse to free 14 detained Gaza doctors

Physicians for Human Rights-Israel says the 14 doctors, some held for more than two years, include hospital directors and specialists.

Disability5 Nov

Three Gaza siblings get wheelchairs built for them

Anas, Mohammad and Sujood Al-Sharif received wheelchairs, writing aids and walking supports made in Gaza by the NGO EducAid with UN funding.

Shelter

Why are 150,000 tents still waiting outside Gaza?

Aid agencies say entry restrictions, slow approvals and a $186.5 million funding gap keep shelter supplies outside while stocks inside run out.

Maternal health

Why do Gaza doctors want a birth-defects inquiry?

Gaza recorded 260 congenital anomalies in nine months of 2026 against 184 in all of 2022, and doctors say the cause has not been established.

Aid

How does aid get into Gaza now?

Most cargo is offloaded at Kerem Shalom; the UN manifested 466 trucks in the last week of October, 28 per cent fewer than the week before.

Education4 Nov

From cart driver back to pupil at 12

A boy who lost his mother in a 2024 strike and left learning to work has returned to school after counselling and family support, OCHA reports.

Ceasefire30 Oct

What is Gaza's 'yellow line'?

It is the line Israeli forces pulled back to under the October 2025 ceasefire; the UN says it is shifting west and treated as a kill zone.

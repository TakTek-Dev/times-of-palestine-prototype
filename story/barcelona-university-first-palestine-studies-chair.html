University of Barcelona launches Spain's first academic chair dedicated to Palestine studies

In one line

The UB-Fundacions Ferrer Chair for Palestine Studies was inaugurated in Barcelona with An-Najah's Sanaa Alsarghali taking part.

On record19 October 2026, 14:31 JerusalemBarcelona2 min read

The University of Barcelona has inaugurated what it describes as the first academic chair in Spain devoted to Palestine studies. The UB-Fundacions Ferrer Chair for Palestine Studies was launched in cooperation with the Fundacions Ferrer group of foundations, WAFA reported on 19 October. The university's rector, Professor Joan Guàrdia, attended the ceremony alongside Palestine's ambassador to Spain, Fadi al-Husseini, the agency said in a dispatch from Barcelona.

The chair is intended to promote Palestine studies in European universities and to support interdisciplinary research on Palestinian history, society, culture and heritage as well as political and legal questions. Guàrdia spoke at the ceremony about the part universities play in advancing knowledge and human rights, and about widening academic cooperation with Palestinian institutions, according to the agency's account of the event.

Dr Sanaa Alsarghali took part in the launch. She is an associate professor of constitutional law at An-Najah National University in Nablus and holds the UNESCO Chair for Human Rights, Democracy and Peace there. She stressed the importance of cooperation between Palestinian and European universities, of support for Palestinian research, and of raising the international profile of Palestinian scholarship and identity.

Alsarghali also drew attention to the conditions under which Palestinian higher education operates and argued that international partnerships are one of the ways to sustain it. The difficulties are practical as well as financial. On 27 October Israeli authorities turned back a 23-member international delegation of parliamentarians and academics at the Allenby crossing as it travelled to a conference at Birzeit University, the university said.

The Barcelona chair arrives as international bodies pay closer attention to Palestinian education. WAFA reported the same day that UNESCO's executive board, meeting in Paris for its 225th session, had unanimously adopted two resolutions: one on the effects of the situation in Gaza on education, science and culture, and one supporting the continuation of schooling provided by the UN agency for Palestine refugees.

Spain recognised the State of Palestine in 2024, and the ambassador's presence at the ceremony gave the launch a diplomatic as well as an academic character. For Palestinians living in Catalonia and for Spanish students, a university chair offers something more durable than a single conference or exhibition: a standing programme whose purpose is to teach, research and publish on Palestine from one academic year to the next.

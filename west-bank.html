Settler violence11 Nov

More than 100 vehicles burned in village south of Nablus

WAFA reports that dozens of settlers torched a scrapyard, cars and olive trees in al-Lubban ash-Sharqiya; losses exceed one million shekels.

Health10 Nov

Qalqilya protests plan to close UNRWA hospital

Protesters want UNRWA to keep its Qalqilya hospital, open since 1951 and serving more than 17,000 refugee families, and reopen two wards.

Education8 Nov

Masafer Yatta: teachers detained, school raided

The UN says ten Jinba school teachers were detained for about five hours on 1 November; a teacher was arrested at Al-Fakhit school on 8 November.

Olive harvest7 Nov

Olive harvest in numbers: a record 377 settler attacks

OCHA counted 377 harvest-related settler attacks between 1 September and 2 November, more than in any previous olive season it has recorded.

Environment6 Nov

760 environmental violations logged in West Bank this year

The Environmental Quality Authority says it logged 760 environmental violations by Israeli forces and settlers from January to September 2026.

Land4 Nov

2,536 dunums near Ramallah declared 'state property'

A 3 November declaration covers land of Beitillu, al-Mazra'a al-Qibliya and Deir Ammar; owners have 45 days to object.

Hebron2 Nov

What happened in Hebron's ten-day lockdown?

From 23 October to 2 November Israeli forces sealed two Hebron neighbourhoods, displacing more than 3,000 people who are now returning.

Bethlehem pupil is runner-up in Arab Reading Challenge

Ghazal Taim, 16, of al-Awda Secondary School for Girls, finished second in the 10th Arab Reading Challenge final in Dubai on 29 October.

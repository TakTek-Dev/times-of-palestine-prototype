about

Nov 11, 2026 | 6:00 PM

The Times of Palestine is an English-language news site about Palestine.

The news of the day, the context behind it, and the stories of the people who live it. We report daily life as carefully as the day's events, and we say where every fact comes from.

How to read us

The three leaves of our mark tell you when a story is from. A red leaf is today. A green leaf is this week. A black leaf is on the record: older, and still true.

Every story opens with its answer in one line, and ends with the sources it was reported from. Questions people ask most are answered in explained.

The desks

Stories are filed by desk: Gaza, West Bank, Jerusalem, Diaspora, Solidarity and Analysis.

Read our editorial standards, or contact the newsroom.
